About Us

eVALeBike is a cutting-edge platform designed to simplify the e-bike testing and repairing process. Our system ensures your bike meets all safety standards before hitting the road.

Monitor your bike’s testing history in real-time.

How It Works

For Customers

Step 1: Visit a Workshop

Bring your e-bike to a certified eVALeBike test center.

Step 2: Technician Registers You

Your account is created on-site by a technician.

Step 3: Receive an Email

You'll get a link to activate your dashboard and access your bike data.

Step 4: Access Your Dashboard

Scan the QR code or log in directly to view your bike's reports.

Step 5: View Your Report

Check detailed diagnostics and safety status anytime.

For Technicians

Step 1: Create an Account

Sign up as a technician on the platform to start testing bikes.

Step 2: Get Approved

Your account will be verified by an admin before use.

Step 3: Run a Test

Connect the bike to the testbench and run diagnostics.

Step 4: Analyze Results

View analytics and send reports directly to the customer's dashboard.
